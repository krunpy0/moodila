# Moodila

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who want to remember their days, notice their moods, and share selected entries with close friends. Mobile use comes first.

## Product Purpose

A personal mood and day journal with an optional friends feed. The landing page invites visitors to create an account. This emphasis was confirmed by the user on 2026-11-06.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Five mood levels, text entries, tags, photos, calendar, mood statistics, friends, comments and reactions. Entries can be hidden globally or from individual friends. This is a React PWA with Russian and English interfaces. Authentication uses the existing /login route. Demonstrations on the landing page are illustrative and do not save entries.

## Brand Commitments

Moodila; the existing logo; the Soft Editorial system in design-reference/DESIGN.md. Warm linen, dusty rose, desaturated mood colors, Plus Jakarta Sans with Manrope for Cyrillic. The user explicitly requires this system and prohibits inspecting the previous landing through GitHub diffs or history.

## Evidence on Hand

Implemented product features, docs/, existing UI components and design-reference/DESIGN.md. No verified customer counts, testimonials, or health outcome claims were supplied.

## Product Principles

- Keep journaling approachable and personal.
- Make the sharing audience clear.
- Show real capabilities with clearly labeled sample content.
- Preserve mobile accessibility and safe-area spacing.
